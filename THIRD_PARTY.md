# Third-party content and trademark notice

## BIAN Semantic APIs — in the `sdem-bian` extension, not in SDEM

Since SDEM 0.22, **SDEM contains no BIAN content.** BIAN's public **Semantic APIs, release 14.0.0**, published by the Banking Industry Architecture Network (BIAN e.V.) at [github.com/bian-official/public](https://github.com/bian-official/public) under the **Apache License 2.0**, ship with the separate [`sdem-bian`](https://github.com/taumoeps/sdem-extensions/tree/main/metamodel/bian) extension, packed into `sdem_bian/data/catalogue/base-14.0.0.json` (names, operations and their fields, control records, behaviour qualifiers; BIAN's descriptions and object schemas left out), with BIAN's licence and a statement of what was changed beside it.

Neither package holds other BIAN material — no BIAN portal or member content, no Service Landscape descriptions, no BIAN text in the documentation. The banking examples' BIAN layers, in `sdem-bian`, are made up.

## Published reference architectures — named, not copied

SDEM's metamodel names concepts from **ArchiMate 3.2** and **TOGAF 10**, and `sdem-bian`'s from **BIAN** (publicly available at bian.org; copyright BIAN e.V.) (published by The Open Group; sign-in required; copyright The Open Group), and describes each in its own words; no text from them is reproduced. ArchiMate relationship tables were checked against **Archi** (github.com/archimatetool/archi, MIT licence), not copied from it. Entity by entity: `docs/reference/reference-architecture-provenance.md`.
